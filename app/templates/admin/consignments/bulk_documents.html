Bulk shipment documents

Upload by consignment number, or download documents together.

Upload PODs and invoices

Save shipments first. Name files consignmentNum_pod.pdf or consignmentNum_invoice.jpg. PDF, JPG, PNG and WebP are accepted. Maximum 50 files, 5 MB each and 50 MB combined.

Uploads are saved immediately. A matching document is replaced; previous versions are retained in the migrated database.

Download a document ZIP

Select shipments using the table checkboxes, across pages if needed. ZIP filenames follow consignmentNum_pod.ext and consignmentNum_invoice.ext. A manifest lists missing documents. Up to 200 shipments and 250 MB per ZIP.
